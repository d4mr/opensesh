This page gets you from zero to a working event: sign in, look around with the demo personas, then create your own workspace and event.

## Sign in

Go to [app.opensesh.io/login](https://app.opensesh.io/login). You can sign in three ways: with a **password**, with a **magic link** (click **Email me a magic link** and open it from your inbox — submitters and speakers use this flow too, so no one has to remember a password), or with one of the one-click **demo personas** below.

## Create a workspace and event

<Steps>
<Step>

### Sign in with your own account

Use a password or a magic link — either works.

</Step>
<Step>

### Create an organization

This is your workspace. Organizations hold members, the speaker CRM, and API keys. Invite teammates from the organization settings dialog (Profile, Members, Invitations); they accept at an `/accept-invitation` link.

</Step>
<Step>

### Create an event inside the organization

Org owners and admins are automatically admins of every event; you can additionally add event members with `admin` or `reviewer` roles.

</Step>
<Step>

### Work through Event Settings

Open **Event Settings** and go section by section: **Basics** (name, slug, dates), **Schedule**, **Branding**, **Submissions**, **Access**, and **Integrations**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Seed the event library

Fill in Tracks, Formats, Rooms, Tags, and Levels under the library settings — the CFP form builder, evaluation, and agenda all bind to these shared lists.

</Step>
</Steps>

From here, follow the lifecycle checklist: build a form in [Call for Papers](/guides/call-for-papers), review what comes in on the [Submissions desk](/guides/submissions), and take accepted talks through [Sessions](/guides/sessions) to a published [Agenda](/guides/agenda).
