title: "Speakers & speaker CRM"

opensesh tracks speakers at two levels: a per-event **Speakers** directory for running _this_ conference, and an organization-wide **Speaker CRM** that persists across events.

## Event speakers: the Spotlight view

**Speakers** (`/admin/speakers`) is a master-detail directory. The table shows **Speaker, Role, Profile readiness, Workflow, Task progress**; selecting a row opens the **Spotlight** detail pane. Each speaker moves through a workflow: `invited` → `onboarding` → `confirmed` → `ready` (or `declined`).

<Callout title="Built for working through a long list fast">
  Navigate with `j` / `k` and close with `Escape`. The URL carries
  `?spotlight=<id>`, so views are shareable.
</Callout>

The Spotlight pane shows:

| Panel                     | What it shows                                                                       |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Awaiting your review**  | Portal edits pending approval, one click from the [Content](/guides/sessions) flow. |
| **Workflow status**       | Where they are in onboarding.                                                       |
| **Profile readiness**     | A checklist of Bio, Headshot, Dietary, T-shirt, and Profile approval.               |
| **Contact and logistics** | The practical details.                                                              |

The toolbar has **Export CSV** and **Import CSV** for bulk work.

## Organization CRM

**Speaker CRM** (`/admin/crm`) is the long-lived contact database for your whole organization, with four tabs:

<Tabs items={["Directory", "Pipeline", "Segments", "Overview"]}>
<Tab>

Columns: **Contact, Title, Company, Tags, Events** (which of your events they've touched). From here you can:

- **Import CSV** — with header mapping, a per-row preview before commit, and dedupe by email so re-imports don't create duplicates.
- **Merge duplicate contacts** — pick the surviving record; history and event links consolidate.
- Start a **campaign** from the current selection (see [Email](/guides/email)).

</Tab>
<Tab>

A stage board for speaker outreach. The seeded stages are **Prospect** and **Contacted** (open), **Confirmed** (won), and **Declined** (lost) — every stage move is recorded in the contact's stage history, so you can see how a relationship developed across events.

</Tab>
<Tab>

**Segments** are saved filters (by tags, stage, event participation) you can reuse for campaigns.

</Tab>
<Tab>

**Overview** shows the health of the database: **Total contacts, Events reached, Open, Lost, Profile complete %**, top companies, and tag distribution.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## How the two levels connect

Event speakers are linked to CRM contacts. Accept someone's talk at one event and next year they're already in the Directory with their history, tags, and pipeline stage — invite them straight from the CRM instead of starting cold. The [Accelevents integration](/guides/integrations) also imports into the CRM, tagging attendees so you can target them as future speakers.
