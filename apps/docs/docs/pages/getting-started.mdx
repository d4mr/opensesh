# Getting started

This page gets you from zero to a working event: sign in, look around with the demo personas, then create your own workspace and event.

## Sign in

Go to [app.opensesh.io/login](https://app.opensesh.io/login). You can sign in three ways:

- **Password** — enter your email and password.
- **Magic link** — click **Email me a magic link** and open the link from your inbox. Submitters and speakers use this flow too, so no one has to remember a password.
- **Demo personas** — one-click sign-in buttons for exploring the seeded demo event.

## Create a workspace and event

1. Sign in with your own account (password or magic link).
2. Create an **organization** — this is your workspace. Organizations hold members, the speaker CRM, and API keys. Invite teammates from the organization settings dialog (Profile, Members, Invitations); they accept at an `/accept-invitation` link.
3. Create an **event** inside the organization. Org owners and admins are automatically admins of every event; you can additionally add event members with `admin` or `reviewer` roles.
4. Open **Event Settings** and work through the sections: **Basics** (name, slug, dates), **Schedule**, **Branding**, **Submissions**, **Access**, and **Integrations**.
5. Seed the **event library** (Tracks, Formats, Rooms, Tags, Levels) under the library settings — the CFP form builder, evaluation, and agenda all bind to these shared lists.

From here, follow the lifecycle checklist: build a form in [Call for Papers](/guides/call-for-papers), review what comes in on the [Submissions desk](/guides/submissions), and take accepted talks through [Sessions](/guides/sessions) to a published [Agenda](/guides/agenda).
